Statement 2: Trend in level of Consumption since 2011-12: All-India. Average MPCE (Rs.): 2011-12 (68th round): Rural 1,430, Urban 2,630. 2022-23: Rural 3,773, Urban 6,459. 2023-24: Rural 4,122, Urban 6,996. Difference as % of Rural MPCE: 83.9 (2011-12), 71.2 (2022-23), 69.7 (2023-24).

[Method note on same page: estimates based on Modified Mixed Reference Period (MMRP): last 7 days for perishables, last 365 days for clothing/education/medical-hospitalisation/durables, last 30 days for all other items.]
