Table 1: Labour Force Participation Rate (in per cent) in usual status (ps+ss) estimated from PLFS 2022, PLFS 2023, PLFS 2024 and PLFS 2025 for persons of age 15 years and above and persons of all ages. PLFS 2025, 15 years and above: rural male 80.5, rural female 45.9, rural person 62.8; urban male 76.2, urban female 27.7, urban person 52.2; all male 79.1, female 40.0, person 59.3. All ages: person 44.9.

[Scope note: 2025 refers to January-December 2025 (likewise 2024/2023/2022) — calendar-year series after the 2025 revamp shifted the cycle from July-June. Do not mix with July-June annual figures without the revamp caveat.]
