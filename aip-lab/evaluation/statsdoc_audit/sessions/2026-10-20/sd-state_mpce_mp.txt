What is the MPCE in mp?

10/20/2026 at 6:47 PM

Based on the provided document, Monthly Per Capita Consumption Expenditure (MPCE) is defined as follows:

Definition: It is the total household monthly consumption expenditure divided by the household size.
Purpose: This measure serves as an indicator of the household's level of living.
Individual Application: An individual's MPCE is defined as the MPCE of the household to which they belong. This assigns a number to each person representing their level of living, though it ignores intra-household allocation among members.
Estimation: It is based on data collected from households that provided information for all three visits (FDQ, CSQ, and DGQ).
