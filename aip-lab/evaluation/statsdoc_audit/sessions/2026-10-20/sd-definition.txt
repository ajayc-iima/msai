What is MPCE in HCES?

10/20/2026 at 6:45 PM

Based on the provided document, MPCE stands for Monthly Per Capita Consumption Expenditure.

The document provides the following details regarding MPCE in the context of HCES (Household Consumption Expenditure Survey):

Definitions and Summaries:
Average MPCE: Refers to the average monthly per capita consumption expenditure by State/UT and sector.
MPCE Fractile Classes: A fractile class of MPCE is a segment of the population lying within two fractiles ($f_1$ and $f_2$). Estimates are generated across different fractile classes to assess variations in characteristics.
Estimation and Imputation:
Standard Estimates: Generated by imputing value figures for consumption from home-grown/home-produced stock, gifts, free collections, and goods received in exchange for goods and services.
MPCE Estimates with Imputation: A separate set of estimates where value figures are imputed for items/services received free of cost through various Social Welfare Schemes (such as specific food items like rice, wheat, pulses, etc., and non-food items like laptops, mobile handsets, and clothing).
PDS Items: From January 2023, PDS items distributed to NFSA beneficiaries were made free, and their value figures were also imputed in the "MPCE estimates with imputation."
Usage Notes:
States/UTs are considered the basic stratum; generating MPCE estimates below the State/UT level (e.g., at the district level) may lead to inaccurate figures due to insufficient sample observations.
