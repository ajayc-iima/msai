Refuse to Hallucinate

A deterministic validity firewall for Indian official survey statistics

Vidhi (विधि, vidhi — the method, the rule, the law) is a validity firewall for Indian official survey statistics. Every question gets a cited verdict — frozen with SHA-256 before any document is read. StatsDoc is MoSPI’s document-search assistant (β): it finds numbers; Vidhi decides whether you may say them.

MoSPI’s tools help you find the number. This firewall checks whether you are allowed to say it — and stops you, with a cited rule, when you are not.

The problem, and the fix

Retrieval over official PDFs is solved. Governance is not. Unconstrained RAG over sovereign statistics fails in three ways — observed live in MoSPI’s StatsDoc pilot (archived transcripts, chats 2472–2480):

✕Inconsistent figures in one answer. Measured MP MPCE quoted as both 3,158 and 5,589 for urban, and 5,538 vs 4,987 for "All", in a single response (chat 2474).
✕Unguided cross-round arithmetic. ~40% MPCE growth quoted across the HCES 2011-12 → 2023-24 break (paper → 3-visit CAPI) with no caveat.
✕Retrieval poisoning. A poisoned document chunk can override guardrails and flip a verdict.
The thesis. You cannot fix a probabilistic system with more probabilistic prompts. The decision — is this claim allowed? — must come from versioned, deterministic rules, before generation. Models translate; rules judge.

Every refusal is cited

A verdict is never bare prose — it carries a stable reason code.

R_OKverified row matched
R_NO_CURATED_ROWnot curated → coverage note, no figure
R_NUMERIC_MISMATCHasserted number ≠ the store
R_COMPARABILITY_*survey break → mandatory caveat
R_SCOPE_OUT / R_FORECASTforecast, causal, political → refused
R_PROMPT_INJECTIONinjection refused before any lookup

Safety-B · freeze before retrieval + figure attestation

The verdict hash is locked before any document is read. A planted "ignore the caveat" chunk changes nothing — the hash is byte-identical with or without poisoning. A second hash binds the number you see to its cell.

frozen_hash = SHA-256(claim ⊕ decision ⊕ sub_code ⊕ reason ⊕ rule_version)
attestation = SHA-256(frozen_hash ⊕ row_id ⊕ value ⊕ unit ⊕ pdf_sha256)

Field-guarded cache · no safe threshold

Danger-pair and paraphrase cosine ranges overlap on both measured embedders, so no similarity threshold is safe. Reuse only on exact critical-field match.

0 collisions across 19 danger pairs · 1708 verified rows (55 touched by the golden set)

Generalised, not hard-coded

The translator resolves every state/UT the store carries, in any spelling — “mp”, “up”, “m.p.”, “orissa”, “maharastra” — and answers one state or several at once. An open slice returns every published cell, never one narrowed figure.

“MPCE in Madhya Pradesh, UP, Maharashtra” → 12 cells · 12 row ids

What stops the three attacks

Prompt injection (EN · Hindi · Hinglish) — S1 refuses before any lookup, with a cited rule. Poisoned document chunk — the verdict hash is already frozen, so the chunk changes prose, never the verdict. Cache poisoning — reuse only on exact critical-field match, so a national row can never answer a state slice.

The store at a glance

  • Sources — PLFS Highlights 2023-24 & 2025, TUS 2024 factsheet & report, HCES 2023-24 factsheet & final report. Four official PDFs, committed and sha256-bound.
  • Rows — 1,708 verified; 1,397 (82%) also carry the PDF page they were read from, and 1,400 of 1,400 re-verify on that page.
  • Rules — version 4; six comparability rules with verbatim MoSPI quotes, and a freshness rule (store as of 2026-10-20).

Honest limits

  • Store as of 2026-10-20 — a later round is not in the store and says so, rather than answering “latest” with a stale row. Curation is the real cost: onboarding a survey is a manual, PDF-anchored job.
  • Coverage is the limit, not logic — the PLFS Highlights battery, the TUS 2024 battery and the HCES 2023-24 state + trend columns. State-wise MPCE covers all 36 states/UTs in any spelling. Uncovered slices return WARN_NO_DATA + a coverage note, never a guess.
  • Heuristic extraction breaks on exotic phrasing; an LLM translator port exists (VIDHI_USE_LLM=1), unmeasured.
  • No load test yet; the eSankhyiki live-API path inherits third-party cert-verification weakness (query path is offline).

What the hash proves. In scope: reproducible and replayable — same question, same hash, and a planted chunk cannot move it. Out of scope: tamper-evidence against someone who holds the store. The attestation binds a number to a row; it does not certify that the row’s reading of the PDF is the only sensible one.
